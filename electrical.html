FAQs

Electrical questions.

What qualifies as an electrical emergency?

Exposed wires, smoke from an electrical device, loss of power, an electrical fire, or any situation where there's a risk of electric shock. Call us immediately, or dial 000 if it involves fire or life-threatening conditions.

Do you install smart home devices?

Yes. We install thermostats, security systems and lighting that can be controlled from your phone or by voice.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, on all labour and parts we provide. Terms depend on the service and parts used, but typically range from 1 to 5 years.
