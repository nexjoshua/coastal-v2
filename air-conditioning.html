Ducted air conditioning

Hidden, quiet, whole-home comfort.

Ducted systems sit out of sight in your ceiling and deliver heating and cooling through discreet vents. Add zoning and you only run the rooms you're using, which keeps running costs down.

Tricky roof? We've fitted ducted systems into homes with almost no ceiling space. Talk to us before you rule it out.

Servicing

A clean system runs better.

Condensation lines need to be properly cleaned to avoid algae and mould building up, which can be hazardous to you and your loved ones.

A regular service keeps your ducted or split system efficient and reliable, and catches small problems before they become expensive ones. Not sure if yours needs one? Try our Aircon 101 tools.
