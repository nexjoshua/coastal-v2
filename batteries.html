Batteries + air con

Cool the evening on stored sunshine.

The hottest part of the day often runs into the evening, right when your solar stops producing. A battery stores the extra power from the afternoon so your air conditioning can keep running on it after sunset.

Because we do the air con too, we can look at your cooling, heating and battery together. Explore air conditioning.

FAQs

Battery questions.

Do I need solar panels to get a battery?

Batteries work best with solar, because they store the extra power your panels make during the day. If you don't have solar yet, give us a call and we'll talk through what makes sense for your home.

Are there rebates for home batteries?

Government battery incentives change from time to time. Ask us and we'll tell you what currently applies to your home.

Who installs the battery?

We work with a trusted partner who specialises in battery installs. We organise everything and stay your point of contact from the first chat to handover.
